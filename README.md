## Research & Publications

### Revolutionizing Early Lung Cancer Detection with AI and ML Solutions

**Independent research preprint** exploring machine learning methods for lung cancer prediction using a publicly available dataset.

- **Methods explored:** Decision Tree, Random Forest, Logistic Regression, and an ensemble approach using the Multiplicative Weight Update Method.
- **Evaluation:** The study discusses classification performance using metrics including accuracy, precision, and recall.
- **Reported result:** The preprint reports 96.62% accuracy for its ensemble approach on the evaluated dataset.
- **Important context:** This is a study-specific result and does not establish clinical effectiveness or generalization to real-world patient populations.

[Read the preprint →](https://research-archive.org/index.php/rars/preprint/view/3512)
